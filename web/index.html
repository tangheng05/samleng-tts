    (03) How it works

    A big teacher,
    a small student

    Large models speak Khmer well, but they are slow and need a GPU. We use one as a teacher to train a small model that runs fast on an ordinary computer.

    1. ខ្ញុំរីករាយណាស់ដែលបានជួបអ្នក។

      9,042sentences read by the teacher

      The teacher reads

      VoxCPM2, a 2-billion-parameter model, reads every sentence in one fixed voice.

    2. One dot = 100 clipsrejected

      574clips thrown out

      Every clip is checked

      A speech recognizer and a native ear reject clips that are misread, off-voice or too fast. 8,468 clips (8.5 hours) are kept.

    3. Teacher · 2B
      Student · 36M

      56×smaller than the teacher

      The student learns the voice

      A 36-million-parameter model learns from the kept clips and runs faster than real time on a regular CPU.

    (05) Lab log

    What we tried,
    including what failed

    Error rate at each checkpoint

    Live · lower is better

    1. 13 Nov 2026

      The big run

      8.5 hours of teacher audio. It crashed twice because the Mac's GPU ran out of memory; we found the cause, fixed it, and resumed from the last checkpoint.

    2. 12 Nov 2026

      First listener scores

      Blind ratings: base model 2.16 → Samleng v1 3.40. Steady speaking pace won both by ear and by the numbers.

    3. 11 Nov 2026

      A new teacher voice

      We picked a clearer synthetic voice and had the teacher re-read every sentence.

    4. 10 Nov 2026

      KhmerTTS-Bench published

      Measured all 7 open Khmer TTS models and two Gemini versions. Gemini 3.8 is the most accurate, but it is a paid service.

    5. 09 Nov 2026

      The first student

      Trained on just 1.2 hours of teacher audio, it made about 43% fewer errors on Khmer sentences.

    6. 06 Nov 2026

      Metrics can mislead

      On Khmer–English sentences, the plain error rate did not match what people hear. We built a new measure and checked it against listener scores.
